| Tier | Projects | Treatment |
|---|---|---|

| **Featured** (home + top of /work) | Open Omaha · KOMPETE · AML Watcher | Standard case study |

| **Collection** | "Game UI collection": Legacy Battle Royal · Taxa's Hold'em · Roulette | One page, gallery-led, 150 words each |
| **Exclude** | Unseen Adventure, website redesigns, Stopshop, Quiz App, logo folios | Not on site; keep on Behance |

Rationale: the hero must prove *systems* (multi-surface, hardware, AI, back-office). The three featured prove *outcome* (Open Omaha +40%), *scale/complexity* (KOMPETE, AML) and cover all three industries you want to be hired in. Secondary proves range without diluting. Games get a collection so the visual proof stays but doesn't outnumber product work.

Balance on home: 2 fintech/SaaS + 2 games? No — **1 hero (fintech) + 3 featured (game, game, SaaS)** reads game-heavy. Swap in Mintavibe for KOMPETE on *home* if KOMPETE facts don't arrive; keep KOMPETE featured on /work.
