1. **Systems + engagement in one designer.** Enterprise complexity (POS, AML, RPA) *and* game engagement loops (retention, conversion, reward systems). Most designers have one; fintech and consumer products increasingly need both (gamified cashback, loyalty, onboarding).

3. **Hardware-aware.** POS terminals and kiosks: touch targets, offline mode, glare, gloves, queue pressure — real-world constraints, not just screens.
4. **Proven remote across time zones** — Canada, USA, Pakistan; 6-year Upwork track record with reviews.
5. **AI-assisted research & workflow** — a modern signal, but subordinate; everyone claims it, so show *how* (one paragraph in About), don't headline it.
