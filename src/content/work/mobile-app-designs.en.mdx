## Four apps, designed screen by screen

These are concept apps rather than client work. There was no brief, no research round and no launch
to measure, so there is no process to walk through and no numbers to quote — what there is, is the
interface, which is the whole of the argument. Each one takes a familiar job and works out where the
decisions go: what the screen asks for, in what order, and what it shows you before you commit.
